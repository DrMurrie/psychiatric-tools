Autism Tests

Self-report screening scales for autism spectrum traits in adults

Patient Information
RAADS-R Instructions

The Ritvo Autism Asperger Diagnostic Scale–Revised (RAADS-R) is an 80-item self-report measure of autistic symptoms in adults. For each statement, choose the option that best describes you. Consider both how you are now and how you were before you were 16. There are no right or wrong answers — answer as honestly as you can.

Response options: TW = True now and when I was young  ·  TN = True only now  ·  TY = True only when I was younger than 16  ·  N = Never true
RAADS-R Total: 0 / 240   —   Not rated

RAADS-R Plain Text Summary

Reference: Ritvo RA, Ritvo ER, Guthrie D, et al. The Ritvo Autism Asperger Diagnostic Scale-Revised (RAADS-R): a scale to assist the diagnosis of autism spectrum disorder in adults: an international validation study. J Autism Dev Disord. 2011;41(8):1076–1089. Total score range 0–240; a total ≥65 is consistent with autism spectrum disorder (reported sensitivity ~97%, specificity ~100% in the validation sample). Four subscales: Language (0–21), Social Relatedness (0–117), Sensory/Motor (0–60), Circumscribed Interests (0–42). Seventeen “normative” items are reverse-scored. The RAADS-R is a screening aid designed for use alongside a clinical interview — it is not a stand-alone diagnostic instrument and does not replace comprehensive assessment by a qualified clinician.
